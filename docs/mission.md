# dofus-overlay

This is a Factory for building a Dofus overlay. 

Dofus is an amazing game. MMORPGs were shining during my 
childhood at a time where there was no better alternatives. 

MMORPGs were fun because online games where seldom at the time.
They allowed players to trade with others, play with their friends
and talk about it most importantly. They were a great source of
fun but very repetitive. The grind was hard. The amount of farm
and idle times was huge. Walking through the maps. Talking to NPCs.
That was not really fun, you would end up blasting through the 
dialogues and just go and kill monsters and eventually experience a
bug or search for an artifact for hours. Progression was also
 very unclear and way to free and broad. Resources would also 
often be contested by many players or even bots. Making playing 
the game a huge investment of time for a little ROI in terms of
fun. PVP was fun at a time where everyone was discovering the game. 
Rapidly PVP became fun for the very few that had the top gears.

So what was fun about them?

- Online Multiplayer: Playing with friends and making friends
- RPG: Discovering the lore and the open world environment
- Gameplay: Fighting monsters, what you were supposed to do
- Crafting system: the core gameplay give you access to resources that
  you can use to craft items and level up your craft skills. 
- Economy: trade resources or crafted items with other players.
  Get reach by providing value to other players and buy gear or
  resources to improve your character or unlock your journey into the
  game.
- Gearing and customization: The grind and the theory crafting
  behind gearing and customizing your passive tree was and is still
  today a very fun part of video games.

What ended this era of MMORPG: free games that focused on gameplay 
and rewarded skills instead of hours of farming. Games such as
league of legends became extremely popular during the early 2010s.

## PVP

Rating: 2/10 for normal players. Probably about 7/10 for top players.

When I played league of legends for the first time, I was astonished.
In one 30 minutes game, I could level my character from 1 to 18,
gear it and fight enemies in PVP fights.
In an MMORPG it would have taken hundreds of hours to level and gear
my character.

## Gameplay

Rank: 7-9/10 depending on the Game. Dofus was a solid 9.

The crux of the MMORPG, its gameplay. MMORPG had relatively fun
gameplay, but Dofus was a beast.

You can team up to 8 character and enter turn-by-turn tactical 
fights against a various set of monsters and bosses mechanics.

Great job. But what is Dofus today? 

Dofus today is mostly a classic MMORPG like WoW. You perform a bunch
of quests and you get some rewards.

Doing quests means clicking/traveling throughout the world and
skipping dialogues as fast as you can.

Question: is it fun? Answer: No.

This "gameplay" is not fun, it's repetitive. You have a browser 
opened on your second screen to speed up and all you want is get
through with it. At the end of the day you are just traveling and
skipping dialogues and eventually have a short break of annoying
quests to do the actual gameplay that is fighting a bunch of 
enemies.

That was fun in 2010. It's not fun anymore. MMORPGs have to adapt.

## RPG

Rating: 6/10. It's interesting at the begining. You get rapidly sick
of it.

Story becomes rapidly repetitive. You might read the dialogues at the
beginning but rapidly skip through it after your first playthrough.

## Conclusion

Reduce as much as possible the number of clicks of the non-gameplay
part of the game.

Focus as much as possible on the actual fun part of the game that
is the gameplay, economy, character/gear customization and grind
towards your objectives.

That's what this project is about: reduce the clicks and spend more
time playing the game and less time traveling or skipping through
dialogues.

Doing the quest yourself is brain-rotting and alienating. I value 
my time to much to spend 90% of my playing time not playing.

# What the project provides

Overlay takes control and performs the brain-rotting part for you.
It clicks through the maps, skip through the dialogues, click on 
buttons at specific time and wait the right time of the day for an
NPC to talk to you.

It's not:
- performing the core gameplay for you: no fight mechanics
- not trading for you
- not farming for you

It's an overlay to help you and reduce the amount of clicks. It's
not a bot-ing software.
